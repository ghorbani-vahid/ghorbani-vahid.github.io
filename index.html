About

Professional profile.

I work at the intersection of systems engineering, distributed systems, intelligent sensing, and computer engineering.

I am a Systems Engineer with a multidisciplinary background in Integrated Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Information Technology. My experience spans systems engineering, distributed and intelligent systems, sensing, data analytics, machine learning, and engineering research.

During my postgraduate studies in South Korea, I contributed to multidisciplinary research and engineering projects involving environmental sensing, data acquisition, databases, machine learning, monitoring systems, and system-level integration.

I am currently a PhD Candidate in Engineering at RMIT University, where my research focuses on robust distributed multi-object tracking and multi-sensor systems, with particular emphasis on reliability, resilience, distributed estimation, and intelligent networked systems.
