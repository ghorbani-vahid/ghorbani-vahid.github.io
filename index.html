Profile

How I work.

I am a quantitative engineer and researcher who enjoys turning ambiguous technical problems into models, experiments, software, and measurable results.

My background spans Computer Engineering, Integrated Engineering, machine learning, mathematical modelling, distributed sensing, data analytics, and scientific research. I am particularly interested in problems where data are noisy, incomplete, uncertain, or difficult to interpret, and where strong reasoning matters as much as model scale.

Across research and applied projects, I have worked with real sensor data, machine-learning systems, Bayesian estimation, anomaly detection, data validation, environmental monitoring, multi-object tracking, and distributed inference. I value solutions that are technically sound, testable, reproducible, and useful outside a purely academic setting.
