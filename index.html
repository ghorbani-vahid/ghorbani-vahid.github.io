Profile

Professional summary.

I turn technically difficult problems into models, algorithms, software, experiments, and measurable results.

My background spans Computer Engineering, Integrated Engineering, machine learning, mathematical modelling, distributed sensing, robotics, computer vision, and scientific research. I am particularly interested in systems where measurements are uncertain, incomplete, noisy, or adversarial, and where reliable decision-making matters.

My work includes robust filtering, intrusion detection, multi-object tracking, re-identification, environmental sensing, missing-data reconstruction, anomaly detection, and research software development. I value solutions that are technically sound, reproducible, testable, and useful beyond a purely academic setting.
